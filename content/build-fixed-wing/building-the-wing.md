## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** The lesson video for this unit is coming soon. Until it is ready, start with the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read these sections of Chapter 4: Wing construction overview, Wing step-by-step instructions, Building the wing spar, Leading edge and gluing the wing, Ailerons and hinges.
3. **Build and sign off.** Build the foamboard wing following the step-by-step instructions, from joining the sheets to the aileron hinges. Then show your instructor the skills listed for sign-off.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare.
