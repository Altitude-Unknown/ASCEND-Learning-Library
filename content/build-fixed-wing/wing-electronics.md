## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the short videos for this unit. If it has none yet, go straight to the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read these sections of Chapter 4: Adding electronics to the wing, Wing band clips.
3. **Build and sign off.** Dry fit the servos, pushrods, and control horns, make the Z-bends with each aileron at neutral, then glue everything in place and fit the wing band clips. Then show your instructor the skills listed for sign-off.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare.
