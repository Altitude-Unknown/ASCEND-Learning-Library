## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the short videos for this unit. If it has none yet, go straight to the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read these sections of Chapter 4: 3D printed parts list, Other materials required, Introduction, Where to start?, Landing gear or no landing gear?, Terminology.
3. **Build and sign off.** Start the 3D prints, beginning with the fuselage because it takes longest, then gather the materials, electronics, and tools from the lists. Then show your instructor the skills listed for sign-off.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare.
