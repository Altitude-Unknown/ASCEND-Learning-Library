## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** The lesson video for this unit is coming soon. Until it is ready, start with the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read these sections of Chapter 4: Elevator / horizontal stabilizer construction, Stitching the plywood hinges, Rudder / vertical stabilizer foamboard construction, Vertical stabilizer and rudder 2 mm plywood construction, V-tail (ruddervator) option, Traditional tail vs. V-tail.
3. **Build and sign off.** After the lab, build the tail surfaces for your airplane in foamboard or plywood. Complete the Aircraft Control Surface Construction lab first. Then show your instructor the skills listed for sign-off.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare.
