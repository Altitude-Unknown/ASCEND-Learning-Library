## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the short videos for this unit. If it has none yet, go straight to the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read these sections of Chapter 4: Assembling the nose, Final assembly of the airplane, Installing the servos and pushrods, Routing wires and testing controls.
3. **Build and sign off.** Assemble the nose and fuselage, glue the tail into its clips, install the tail servos and pushrods, route the wires, and attach the wing. Then show your instructor the skills listed for sign-off.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare.
