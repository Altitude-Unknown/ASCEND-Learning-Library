## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** The lesson video for this unit is coming soon. Until it is ready, start with the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read these sections of Chapter 4: Testing the controls, Transmitter settings, Installing the propeller.
3. **Build and sign off.** Without the propeller, check every control against the chart, set your transmitter, then install the propeller only when you are outside and ready to fly. Then show your instructor the skills listed for sign-off.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare. Finish with the full skills checksheet.
