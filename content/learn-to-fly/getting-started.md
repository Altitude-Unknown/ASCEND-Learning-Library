## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** The lesson video for this unit is coming soon. Until it is ready, start with the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read the Getting started, Landings, and Flight simulators sections of Chapter 3.
3. **Apply your understanding.** Complete the airplane simulator lab, then keep practicing in the simulator until you can fly a full circle without crashing.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare. Return to the reading for any question you missed.
