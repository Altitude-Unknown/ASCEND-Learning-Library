## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the short videos for this unit. If it has none yet, go straight to the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Review any Chapter 3 section where a checkride shows a weak skill.
3. **Apply your understanding.** Fly each checkride with your instructor. Repeat the fifth flight as needed until every skill reaches Mastery.
4. **Check and revisit.** Use the skills checksheet to confirm you can do every skill before your solo.
