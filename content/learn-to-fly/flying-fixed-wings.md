## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the short videos for this unit. Watch the Flight Lab RC setup video first to install the simulator you will use in the lab.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read the Getting started, Landings, and Flight simulators sections of Chapter 3, and follow the [Flight Lab RC setup example](/learn-to-fly/textbook/piloting-remote-aircraft/#example-set-up-flight-lab-rc) to connect your transmitter and calibrate the sticks.
3. **Apply your understanding.** Complete the airplane simulator lab, then keep practicing in the simulator until you can fly a full circle without crashing.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare. Return to the reading for any question you missed.
