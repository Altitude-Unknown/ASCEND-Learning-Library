## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** The lesson video for this unit is coming soon. Until it is ready, start with the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read the Betaflight sections of Chapter 5 with your quadcopter and transmitter beside you, from installing Betaflight through checking the motor direction. Keep the propellers off.
3. **Apply your understanding.** Complete the Betaflight lab, then show your instructor the skills listed for sign-off.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare. The full skills checksheet for the chapter is at the end of this unit.
