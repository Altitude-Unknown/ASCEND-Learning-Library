<h2 id="people">People and responsibilities</h2>

ASCEND brings together teams from **two-year community colleges and four-year universities**. Faculty mentors lead the student teams. University pod leads help run the project and support small groups of participating teams.

### Student teams

Students help shape investigations, build and integrate platforms, collect measurements, evaluate data, and communicate results. Teams can enter with different levels of experience and contribute through engineering, observations, analysis, or several of these activities.

### Faculty mentors

Faculty mentors guide their institution’s team and connect its work to student learning and research goals. They work with pod leads to identify training needs, develop team capability, and support the progression from practice activities to scientific investigations.

### University pod leads

Pod leads coordinate and mentor groups of teams, provide technical support in their areas of expertise, and connect teams with additional specialists. They help teams apply shared science goals, data collection procedures, and quality practices.

A pod lead does not need expertise in every platform. Cross-pod collaboration gives teams access to technical knowledge elsewhere in the network, while experienced teams can support newer participants.

### Project leadership and subject matter experts

Project leadership coordinates the network, shared resources, and project-wide communication. Subject matter experts contribute to research questions, measurement methods, data interpretation, and student mentoring. Their involvement also helps connect students with scientific communities and career pathways.

<h2 id="pods">Technical support and regional collaboration</h2>

The pod model connects teams in two complementary ways:

- **Platform-focused support:** training and technical guidance for radiosondes, high-altitude balloons, UAS, and related data workflows. Expertise can be shared across geographic regions.
- **Regional collaboration:** coordination among nearby teams for complementary measurements and joint investigations tied to the [shared research questions](/science/).

Teams can therefore receive help with a specific instrument or platform while also working with regional partners on a common science problem.

<h2 id="workflow">From training to research</h2>

The project’s planned progression builds skills before larger coordinated investigations:

1. **Identify a starting point.** Faculty mentors, students, and pod leads assess current experience, learning needs, and possible contributions.
2. **Build foundational skills.** Use training resources for platforms, sensor integration, flight preparation, data analysis, and scientific communication.
3. **Develop and test systems.** Integrate instruments, practice operations, and compare measurements with reference observations.
4. **Join coordinated investigations.** Work with pod partners to plan complementary observations around a shared question.
5. **Clean, evaluate, and analyze data.** Document data quality and measurement limitations, compare platforms, and interpret results with mentors and experts.
6. **Share findings and build lasting capability.** Communicate through maps, reports, posters, presentations, and other appropriate scientific formats. Retain reusable tools and partnerships for future student teams.

Participation is intended to be flexible. A team focused on data analysis can contribute alongside a team building an aircraft or flying a balloon payload.

<h2 id="data">Sharing data and results</h2>

ASCEND plans to make quality-controlled observational data accessible beyond the participating teams. Planned outputs include shared flight datasets, collaborative geographic information system (GIS) maps, and open research products accompanied by documentation.

Data cleaning and quality checks are part of that process. A flight measurement becomes more useful when others can understand how it was collected, what it represents, and where its limitations lie.

The data repositories and campaign map are planned services and are not yet connected here. This Learning Library currently provides training and technical resources to help teams prepare.
