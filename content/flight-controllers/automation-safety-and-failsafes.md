## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the short videos for this unit. If it has none yet, go straight to the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read the sections of Chapter 6 on automating a mission, safety around flight-controlled aircraft, and failsafes.
3. **Apply your understanding.** Brief the failsafes and safety procedures of an aircraft you fly, and find any answers you don’t know before you fly it.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare. Return to the reading for any question you missed.
