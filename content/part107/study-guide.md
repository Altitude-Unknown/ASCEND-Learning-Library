## How to use these materials

The supplied **FAA Remote Pilot Test Prep** book identifies itself as the 2026 edition. Its five chapters include objectives, knowledge checks, answer keys, and source notes. All five are published as web chapters below.

The study guide notes updates relative to some of the original slide language.


## Read the chapters online

Follow the course sequence below. The original textbook chapter numbers are retained. Each webpage includes the full chapter, knowledge check, answer key, tables, and figures. The web chapters are kept current as the book is revised, so PDF and Word downloads are no longer offered.
