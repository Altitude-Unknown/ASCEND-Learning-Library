Use these quizzes alongside the [five-unit learning path](/part107/). Choose 10 questions from one unit, all available questions in that unit, or the comprehensive mixed practice exam with 50 questions—10 per unit. Figure questions include the chart or weather report you need, with a link to open it full size.

## Study alongside your practice

Return to the [study guide](/part107/study-guide/), [airspace references](/part107/airspace/), or [weather references](/part107/weather/) when you want to revisit a topic. Consult the [FAA’s study-material guidance](https://www.faa.gov/faq/where-can-i-find-study-materials-part-107-aeronautical-knowledge-test) and [current Part 107 rules](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-107) for official references.

The supplied Whisky Alpha question banks were adapted for ASCEND and checked for question structure, figure links, duplicates, and editorial errors on September 24, 2026. Identified ambiguous questions are excluded from scoring. This review is not FAA validation or a guarantee that every answer is error-free.
