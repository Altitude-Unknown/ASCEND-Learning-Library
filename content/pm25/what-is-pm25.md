## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the lesson video for this unit. If it has none yet, go straight to the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read the What PM2.5 is, Size classes, Where PM2.5 comes from, and Why PM2.5 matters sections of the chapter.
3. **Apply your understanding.** Work the sources activity: list the PM2.5 sources around your launch site and predict which dominate at different times.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare. Return to the reading for any question you missed.
