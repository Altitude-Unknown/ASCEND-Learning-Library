## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the lesson video for this unit. If it has none yet, go straight to the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read the The analysis workflow, Standard figures, and Suggested tools sections of the chapter.
3. **Apply your understanding.** Combine your drone, balloon, and ground data in the synthesis report and presentation.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare. Return to the reading for any question you missed.
