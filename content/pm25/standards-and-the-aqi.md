## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the lesson video for this unit. If it has none yet, go straight to the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read the The PM2.5 standards, The standard in court, The Air Quality Index, and The AQI and airborne data sections of the chapter.
3. **Apply your understanding.** Work the AQI exercise: calculate the AQI by hand for real days near you and compare with the reported values.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare. Return to the reading for any question you missed.
