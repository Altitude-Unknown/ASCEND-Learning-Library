## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the short videos for this unit. If it has none yet, go straight to the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read the sections of Chapter 7 on action, 360°, and multispectral cameras, automating cameras with a Raspberry Pi, and artificial intelligence cameras.
3. **Apply your understanding.** Choose a camera for each of three missions and sketch where you would mount it.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare. Return to the reading for any question you missed.
