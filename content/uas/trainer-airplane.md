## Start with the build guide

To build it step by step with lessons, review questions, and instructor sign-off, take the [Building a Fixed-Wing UAS course](/build-fixed-wing/).

Mike Walach’s illustrated instructions cover the parts list, foamboard wing, conventional and V-tail options, optional landing gear, and final assembly. The project combines printed parts with foamboard, plywood, and a carbon-fiber tube.

## Find the right files

- **64D TPU:** fuselage (standard or stretched), nose, canopy, servo brackets, empennage clips, and landing gear.
- **PETG:** motor mounting, wing supports and clips, a control horn, and tail clips (including 45° V-tail clips).
- **DXF:** two patterns for the horizontal and vertical tailfeathers.
- **Fusion:** editable designs for most printed parts, plus the original `AU-Assembly.f3z` archive.

- **Bench test cradle:** holds the airplane on a table for motor and flight-controller tests with the propeller on.

Follow the guide’s material assignments. Original filenames are retained in every download list.

## Construction and setup videos

The supporting videos cover motor and ESC connections, ESC programming, receiver connections, Spektrum radio setup and binding, and a Venom battery charger demonstration. Choose the clips that match your equipment alongside the written guide.
