## Design files by material

The supplied collection is organized into PETG, 64D TPU, 95A TPU, and foam-tail groups. It includes mounts named for GPS, Pixhawk, and Yellowstone, along with the original `Plane v3.f3z` assembly.

The foam-tail group also includes `v3 Foam Tail.f3d`. Keep the material groups separate when preparing parts.

## Assembly documentation

No separate research-aircraft build guide was included with these files. The [Remote and Autonomous Aircraft Systems lab manual](/resources/remote-aircraft-textbook/) provides broader UAS learning material; the [trainer build guide](/resources/trainer-build-instructions/) belongs to the trainer project.
