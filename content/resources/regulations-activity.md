## About this resource

Apply regulatory research to ten scenarios involving pilot responsibility, night operations, multiple aircraft, incidents, and waivers.

Work through the supplied prompts individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions used in your answers.

## Notes for using the supplied material

Several scenarios intentionally leave room for discussion. Identify missing facts before reaching a conclusion; the injury-reporting scenario does not fully describe the injuries. No answer key was supplied.
