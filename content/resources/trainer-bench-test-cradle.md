## About this cradle

The bench test cradle holds the assembled trainer on a table, so you can run the motor with the propeller on. Use it to check the motor, ESC, and propeller, and to test the flight controller's response with the motor running, without the airplane moving.

It is one printed part. The fuselage sits in the cradle, and the wing rests against the two uprights, which stop the airplane from moving forward when the propeller pulls.

## Setting up a bench test

1. **Clamp the cradle to a sturdy table** with two clamps. Place it so the propeller is past the table edge and clears it.
2. **Strap the fuselage into the cradle** with rubber bands, and fit the wing as you would for flight.
3. **Keep everyone out of the propeller's arc.** Stand behind or beside the airplane, never in front of it or in line with the propeller.
4. **Start at low throttle.** Keep the transmitter in hand, ready to cut the throttle immediately.
