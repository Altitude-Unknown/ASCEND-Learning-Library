## About this activity

**Goal:** complete first flights with a quadcopter (multirotor) in the simulator. Become familiar with all flight controls and attempt take-offs, landings, and flights around the flying area.

Work through this activity on your own or with your team, then show your results to your instructor. Write your answers on paper or in your own notes.

## By the end of this session you should be able to

<ul class="skills-checklist">
<li>Identify and describe the function of each of the four flight controls.</li>
<li>Describe yaw, pitch, roll, and throttle as they pertain to multirotor operation.</li>
<li>Perform a take-off and maintain flight for 5 seconds or more.</li>
<li>Practice flying around the field in a circle.</li>
<li>Attempt a landing if possible.</li>
<li>Fly away from yourself (nose pointing away from you) and back to yourself (nose pointing toward you).</li>
</ul>
