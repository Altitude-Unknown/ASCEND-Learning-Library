## About this resource

An image-based Dallas–Fort Worth airspace handout for classroom chart interpretation.

Work through the supplied prompts individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions used in your answers.

## Notes for using the supplied material

This document consists of chart imagery with no extractable body text. The PDF preserves that imagery; a complete text alternative has not been supplied. Use it as a historical classroom handout, not for navigation.
