## About this activity

**Goal:** practice the basics of foamboard construction, servo installation, and pushrod and control horn linkages. Become proficient in transmitter and receiver setup, servo reversing, dual rates, and expo.

**Procedure:** construct the tail assembly according to the blueprints below. Make a [hot glue hinge](/build-fixed-wing/textbook/building-a-fixed-wing-aircraft/#ailerons-and-hinges) on the rudder and a tape hinge on the elevator. Install servos, control horns, and pushrods to a mechanically neutral position. Bind a transmitter and receiver and power them with an ESC and battery. Set dual rates and expo for both control surfaces, assigned to a switch, for 50/75/100% rates and 40% expo in all positions.

Work on your own or with your team, then have your instructor score your tail with the score sheet at the end.

## Part 2: Servo, control horn, and pushrod assembly

Complete your empennage assembly by installing two servos, two control horns, two pushrods, a 3D-printed servo mount, a 3D-printed tail mount, and a 10 mm × 10 mm carbon-fiber tail boom as shown below. Print the tail mount, servo mount, and control horns from the [64D TPU parts](/resources/trainer-tpu-parts/) and [PETG parts](/resources/trainer-petg-parts/) files.

## Part 3: Transmitter setup

Bind your transmitter to the receiver, check that each control surface moves in the correct direction (reverse any that do not), and assign dual rates and expo to a switch. See [Transmitter settings](/build-fixed-wing/textbook/building-a-fixed-wing-aircraft/#transmitter-settings) in Chapter 4 for the recommended values.
