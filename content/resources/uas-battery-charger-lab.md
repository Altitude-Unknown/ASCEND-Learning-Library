## About this activity

**Goal:** become acquainted with the operation of the battery charging system.

Follow [Charging a battery](/uas-hardware/textbook/hardware-and-electronics/#charging-a-battery) in Chapter 2. Never leave a charging LiPo battery unattended, and charge it in a fire-resistant place.

## By the end of this session you should be able to

<ul class="skills-checklist">
<li>Connect a battery to the charger.</li>
<li>Identify the charging connections and charge control connections.</li>
<li>Properly power the battery charging unit with an AC or DC source.</li>
<li>Identify the battery type.</li>
<li>Determine the correct settings to safely charge the battery.</li>
<li>Locate and navigate all the menus on the charger.</li>
<li>Enable or disable audible tones on the charger.</li>
<li>Determine charge current and voltage.</li>
<li>Charge a battery.</li>
<li>Explain safe charging procedures.</li>
</ul>
