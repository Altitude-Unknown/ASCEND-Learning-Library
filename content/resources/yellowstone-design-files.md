## About this resource

Fusion electronics archive, EAGLE schematic, and board layout for studying or adapting the supplied YELLOWSTONE design.

Use the related YELLOWSTONE resources together when studying the board or planning a payload.

## Notes for using the supplied material

The files share the supplied Walach_Aerospace_CubeSat basename. BRD and SCH are EAGLE XML files; the F3Z file is a Fusion archive. Open them with compatible CAD software. These are supplied design sources, not a manufacturing approval or a tested revision claim.
