## About these files

These are the editable Autodesk Fusion designs behind the trainer's printed parts. Open them in Fusion to change a part, then export a new STL for printing. If you only want to print the parts as designed, use the ready-made [64D TPU](/resources/trainer-tpu-parts/) and [PETG](/resources/trainer-petg-parts/) STL files instead.

- **Fuselage, lid, and nose:** the fuselage (`Fuselag.f3z` archive), the fuselage lid, and the nose (`Nose.f3z` archive).
- **Motor and servos:** the motor mount and nut, the servo mount, and the control horn.
- **Bench test cradle:** `Test-Mount.f3d`, which holds the airplane for motor tests. See the [bench test cradle](/resources/trainer-bench-test-cradle/) page.
- **Wing:** the wing support and the front and rear wing-band clips.
- **Tail:** empennage clips for the foam tail, the 3 mm plywood tail, and V-tails at 30° and 45° (including a 45° foam version).

The designs were updated on November 14, 2026; the earlier canopies, stretched fuselage, 2 mm plywood clips and the `AU-Assembly.f3z` archive were retired. These downloads preserve the original file contents and filenames. Visit the [aircraft project page](/uas/trainer-airplane/) for the related components.
