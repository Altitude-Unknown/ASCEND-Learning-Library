## About this activity

**Goal:** operate a multimeter to measure voltage, and test for shorts and opens.

**Materials:** a multimeter, flight batteries, wires, and wire cutters.

Work through this activity on your own or with your team, then show your results to your instructor. Write your answers on paper or in your own notes.

## By the end of this session you should be able to

<ul class="skills-checklist">
<li>Use a digital multimeter to measure voltage of a DC or AC power source.</li>
<li>Use a digital multimeter to check continuity of a conductor using the resistance or continuity setting.</li>
<li>Use a battery tester to inspect multi-cell LiPo batteries.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Instructor sign-off:</strong> demonstrated to instructor ________ (instructor signs here).</p>

## Part 1: Battery voltage

Using your multimeter, test the voltage of several flight batteries.

<ol class="activity-questions">
<li>What is the voltage of a fully charged flight battery?</li>
<li>What is the voltage of a flight battery that has been used for flight?</li>
<li>How many cells were in the battery you measured?</li>
<li>What is the typical voltage per cell for this type of battery?</li>
</ol>

## Part 2: Continuity

Using the resistance or continuity setting, test for continuity between the ends of a length of wire. Repeat the process with a broken wire.

<ol class="activity-questions" start="5">
<li>How could this test be helpful when troubleshooting a circuit that is not working?</li>
<li>What is the difference between a short and an open?</li>
</ol>
