## About this activity

**Goal:** build beginning proficiency with multiple solder and crimp techniques used to build and repair drones.

**Materials:** safety glasses, solder, flux, PCBs, wire, wire strippers, wire cutters, crimp tool, crimps, crimp housings, heat shrink, and a heat gun or rework tool.

Wear safety glasses. Complete each step and get the instructor to sign off before moving on to the next step.

## Steps

<ol class="activity-questions">
<li>Cut a 12-inch length of wire and strip the insulation from both ends. Perform an in-line splice as demonstrated by the instructor. Instructor signature ________</li>
<li>Cut off your splice from step 1 and repeat the process. This time apply heat shrink to the wire splice. Instructor signature ________</li>
<li>Solder a wire to a through-hole connection on a PCB. Instructor signature ________</li>
<li>Solder a wire to a surface mount pad on a PCB. Instructor signature ________</li>
<li>Solder a component to a PCB using a solder station and a microscope. Instructor signature ________</li>
<li>Remove a component from a PCB using a rework tool. Instructor signature ________</li>
<li>Wash and rinse a PCB using the technique demonstrated by the instructor. Instructor signature ________</li>
<li>Cut and strip a wire for a crimp connector as demonstrated by the instructor. Crimp the stripped end. Instructor signature ________</li>
<li>Insert the crimped connector into the provided crimp housing. Instructor signature ________</li>
</ol>

## Bonus

The task below is not for everyone. However, if you want to further your skills and there are boards to be built, you can try your hand at assembling a full printed circuit board.

If materials and equipment are available, assemble a PCB for the lab using either hand soldering or the solder paste screening method. This may require you to work outside of class time, as it is a long process that requires the microscope and solder station.
