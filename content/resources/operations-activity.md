## About this resource

Practice aviation communication, airport interpretation, lost-link planning, and hazardous-attitude recognition.

Work through the supplied prompts individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions used in your answers.

## Notes for using the supplied material

The example tail number is supplied as N19GWJ; treat it as a fictional classroom identifier. The runway-03 question needs a precise aircraft position and chart orientation to resolve the direction question. Research current airport information for any real mission.
