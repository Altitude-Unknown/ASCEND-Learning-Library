## About this activity

Practice aviation radio communication, airport interpretation, lost-link planning, and recognizing hazardous attitudes.

Answer on paper, in your own notes, or in the Word file below. Work individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions behind each answer.

## Before you start

The example tail number N19GWJ is a fictional classroom identifier. The runway 03 question needs a precise aircraft position and chart orientation to resolve the direction question. Research current airport information for any real mission; the airport details below are from September 2024.

## Questions

Questions 1 and 2: the tail number of an airplane is N19GWJ. Given this tail number, what is the correct phraseology for proper radio communications in each scenario below?

<ol class="activity-questions">
<li>Bozeman ATC (Big Sky Approach) asks you for your current altitude. Your altimeter reads 4,800 feet MSL.</li>
<li>You are making a cold call to Big Sky Approach.</li>
<li>You are operating near Three Forks airport. You have a handheld air-band radio to monitor for air traffic. What frequency should you monitor?</li>
</ol>
