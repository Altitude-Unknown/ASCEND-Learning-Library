## About this activity

**Goal:** complete first flights with an airplane in the simulator. Become familiar with all flight controls and attempt take-offs, landings, and flights around the flying area.

Work through this activity on your own or with your team, then show your results to your instructor. Write your answers on paper or in your own notes.

You need a flight simulator, such as [Flight Lab RC](/software/), and a transmitter or gamepad.

## By the end of this session you should be able to

<ul class="skills-checklist">
<li>Identify and describe the function of each of the four flight controls.</li>
<li>Describe yaw, pitch, roll, and throttle as they pertain to airplane operation.</li>
<li>Perform a take-off and maintain flight for 5 seconds or more.</li>
<li>Practice flying around the field in a circle.</li>
<li>Attempt a landing if possible.</li>
</ul>

<ol class="activity-questions" start="2">

<li>Which control surface on the airplane controls pitch?</li>
