## About this activity

**Goal:** check a launch site and predicted flight path against the Part 101 rules that depend on location: §§ 101.5, 101.33(a), and 101.33(d).

**Materials:** a planned launch site and a flight prediction for it, and either the FAA sectional chart for the area (from the [FAA VFR Raster Charts](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/flight_info/aeronav/digital_products/vfr/) page) or [ASCEND Flight Control](/software/).

Read [Rules for every balloon](/part101/textbook/part-101-for-high-altitude-balloons/#rules-for-every-balloon) and [Checking your launch site and path](/part101/textbook/part-101-for-high-altitude-balloons/#checking-the-map) first.

## Setting up ASCEND Flight Control (optional)

1. On the **Mission** tab, click the map at your launch site and at a few points along the predicted path, so they show on the map.
2. Open **Layers ▾** and choose **VFR sectional chart**.
3. Turn on **Controlled airspace (Class B, C, D, E surface)** and **Special use airspace (prohibited, restricted, MOA)**.
4. Right-click your launch site and choose **What airspace is here?**

These layers are for planning only. They come from FAA data, but the FAA chart and ATC have the final word, and temporary restricted areas are not charted.

<ol class="activity-questions">
<li>If your launch site is inside an airport surface area, what would you need before flying below 2,000 feet above the surface there?</li>
<li>If the path crosses a restricted area, whose permission would you need, and which Subpart D rules would apply inside it?</li>
<li>A sectional chart shows no special use airspace on your path. Why isn’t that enough to be sure there is none?</li>
<li>Would moving the launch site a few miles change any of your answers? Where would you move it?</li>
</ol>

<details>
<summary>Check your answers to questions 1–3</summary>
<ol>
<li>ATC authorization (§ 101.33(a)).</li>
<li>Permission from the using or controlling agency (§ 101.5). Inside a restricted area, Subpart D asks only for § 101.33(d) and (e), plus any limits that agency sets (§ 101.31).</li>
<li>Temporary restricted areas are not charted (AIM 3-4-1), and a chart doesn’t show changes made after it was printed.</li>
</ol>
</details>
