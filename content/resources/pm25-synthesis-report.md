## About this project

**Goal:** answer one research question with your own calibrated data, and communicate it like a scientist.

**Time:** two to three weeks.

Read [The analysis workflow](/pm25/textbook/measuring-pm25-from-the-sky/#workflow) through [Suggested tools](/pm25/textbook/measuring-pm25-from-the-sky/#tools) first, and use the figures and uncertainties from your earlier labs.

## Choose a research question

Pick one, or write your own and check it with your mentor:

- How deep is the morning inversion at our site, and how fast does it break up after sunrise?
- Does PM2.5 measured by the drone at 400 feet track the nearest ground monitor, or is it cut off from the surface at night?
- Was the smoke over our area on the flight day in the boundary layer, aloft, or both? Where did it come from (use HYSPLIT)?
- How much does the RH correction change the profile on a humid morning?

## Report structure

1. Abstract (150 words)
2. Introduction and research question
3. Methods: site, platforms, sensors, calibration, and flight plans
4. Results: the standard figures, with uncertainties
5. Discussion: what the profiles show, comparison with other data, and limitations
6. Conclusion and future work
7. Data availability statement: where the raw and processed data are kept

Then give a 10-minute presentation of your results.
