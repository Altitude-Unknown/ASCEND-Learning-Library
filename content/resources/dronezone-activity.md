## About this resource

Explore the FAA DroneZone workflow and research registration, waivers, and FAA-Recognized Identification Areas.

Work through the supplied prompts individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions used in your answers.

## Notes for using the supplied material

Use this as a guided research activity. Account screens, fees, eligibility, and application procedures can change; confirm them on the FAA site. Creating an account, paying a fee, or submitting an application is not required simply to review this resource.
