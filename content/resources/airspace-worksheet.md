## About this resource

Plan cell-tower inspections using the supplied marked chart and discuss airspace access and flight constraints.

Work through the supplied prompts individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions used in your answers.

## Notes for using the supplied material

The prompt refers to four towers, but only markers 1–3 are visible in the supplied chart. A faculty mentor should identify the intended fourth location or limit the exercise to the visible markers. Use the marked chart included in the worksheet. The locations and chart are classroom examples, not current operational guidance.
