## About this activity

Plan cell-tower inspections using the marked sectional chart below and discuss airspace access and flight constraints.

Answer on paper, in your own notes, or in the Word file below. Work individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions behind each answer.

## Before you start

The locations and chart are classroom examples, not current operational guidance. Check a current sectional chart and the FAA’s current airspace authorization process before planning a real mission.

## The job

You have been hired by Verizon to take aerial imagery of four of their cell towers. The towers are marked on the sectional chart below in green, with a white number marking each tower. All of the towers are 200 feet tall (AGL). As part of the survey, they have asked you to examine the obstructions in the area, so they want a maximum-altitude image directly above each tower in addition to a 360° survey of each tower.

**Materials:** this worksheet and the AutoPylot for Drones app.

## Your task

Examine each of the locations that you need to image, and determine:

<ol class="activity-questions">
<li>Can you legally fly this mission under Part 107 for each tower in question?</li>
<li>If you can’t fly the mission for a particular tower, what are the issues?</li>
<li>If there are towers that need approvals from the FAA, what is the process for gaining approval?</li>
</ol>
