## About this resource

Explore bank angle, load factor, aircraft controls, runway slope, stalls, and spins.

Work through the supplied prompts individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions used in your answers.

## Notes for using the supplied material

The first prompt switches between 1 lb and 1.5 lb and gives battery mass rather than total aircraft weight. Agree on the intended values and flight assumptions with your faculty mentor before calculating. Use aircraft-specific documentation when discussing stall or spin recovery.
