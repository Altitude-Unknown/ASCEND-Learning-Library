## About this activity

Explore bank angle, load factor, aircraft controls, runway slope, stalls, and spins.

Answer on paper, in your own notes, or in the Word file below. Work individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions behind each answer.

## Before you start

The first question switches between 1 lb and 1.5 lb and gives battery weight rather than total aircraft weight. Agree on the intended values and flight assumptions with your faculty mentor before calculating. Use aircraft-specific documentation when discussing stall or spin recovery.

## Questions

<ol class="activity-questions">
<li>You normally fly your sUAS with a 1.5 lb battery, but you have just upgraded it to a 2 lb battery. What was the load factor at 1 lb in a 60-degree bank compared to the load factor with your new battery in a 60-degree bank?<br>Load factor at 1.5 lb: ______<br>Load factor at 2 lb: ______</li>
<li>What happens to the load factor on an aircraft as you bank past 60 degrees?</li>
<li>Which control surface controls yaw? Pitch? Roll?</li>
<li>What is the purpose of flaps?</li>
</ol>

<li>What are some methods for determining the altitude of an sUAS if you don’t have an altimeter installed?</li>
<li>The runway you are launching from has an uphill slope. How will this affect your takeoff? How will it affect landing?</li>
