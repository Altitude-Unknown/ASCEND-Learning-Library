## About this activity

**Goal:** decide whether a balloon is covered by Part 101 using the four tests of 14 CFR 101.1(a)(4).

**Materials:** a calculator. For Part 2, your team’s payload packages, a scale, and a ruler.

Read [What Part 101 covers](/part101/textbook/part-101-for-high-altitude-balloons/#what-part-101-covers) through [Working out the weight/size ratio](/part101/textbook/part-101-for-high-altitude-balloons/#weight-size-ratio) first. Remember: one “yes” on any test is enough. There are 16 ounces in a pound. Write your answers on paper or in your own notes, then open each answer to compare.

## Part 1: Sample payloads

For each balloon, check tests (i) to (iv) and decide whether Part 101 applies. Show your weight/size ratio work.

<ol class="activity-questions">
<li>One package, 3 pounds 8 ounces, in a box 6 × 6 × 6 inches. Suspension line separates with less than 50 pounds of force.</li>
<li>One package, 4 pounds 8 ounces, in a box 4 × 5 × 8 inches. Suspension line separates with less than 50 pounds of force.</li>
<li>One package, 6 pounds 4 ounces, in a box 10 × 10 × 10 inches. Suspension line separates with less than 50 pounds of force.</li>
<li>Two packages: a 5-pound box 8 × 8 × 8 inches and a 5-pound box 7 × 8 × 9 inches. Suspension line separates with less than 50 pounds of force.</li>
<li>Three packages of 4 pounds 4 ounces each, every box 8 × 8 × 10 inches. Suspension line separates with less than 50 pounds of force.</li>
<li>One package, 2 pounds, in a box 4 × 4 × 4 inches, hung from a line that needs more than 50 pounds of impact force to separate.</li>
</ol>

<details>
<summary>Check your answers</summary>
<ol>
<li><strong>Not covered.</strong> 56 ounces. Not over 4 pounds, so test (i) can’t apply; not over 6 pounds; one package; line under 50 pounds.</li>
<li><strong>Covered, test (i).</strong> 72 ounces ÷ (4 × 5 = 20 square inches) = 3.6 ounces per square inch. Over 4 pounds and over 3 ounces per square inch.</li>
<li><strong>Covered, test (ii).</strong> Over 6 pounds. (Its ratio, 100 ÷ 100 = 1.0, doesn’t matter: one test is enough.)</li>
<li><strong>Not covered.</strong> Box 1: 80 ÷ 64 = 1.25. Box 2: 80 ÷ (7 × 8 = 56) ≈ 1.43. Neither ratio is over 3; neither box is over 6 pounds; the total, 10 pounds, is not over 12; line under 50 pounds.</li>
<li><strong>Covered, test (iii).</strong> 3 × 4.25 = 12.75 pounds, more than 12 pounds in two or more packages. (Each ratio is 68 ÷ 64 ≈ 1.06, so test (i) does not apply.)</li>
<li><strong>Covered, test (iv).</strong> The weight is small, but the suspension needs more than 50 pounds of impact force to separate the payload.</li>
</ol>
</details>
