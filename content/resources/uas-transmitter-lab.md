## About this activity

**Goal:** become acquainted with the operation of the transmitter.

Work through the transmitter sections of [Chapter 2](/uas-hardware/textbook/hardware-and-electronics/#transmitters) with your transmitter in hand, then show your instructor each skill.

## By the end of this session you should be able to

<ul class="skills-checklist">
<li>Install the batteries into the radio transmitter.</li>
<li>Access all menus.</li>
<li>Create a new aircraft in the radio.</li>
<li>Change the aircraft type.</li>
<li>Set the display picture.</li>
<li>Set dual rates and expo.</li>
<li>Bind to a receiver.</li>
<li>Explain the operation of all the flight control sticks.</li>
<li>Use the radio to operate an aircraft or simulator.</li>
</ul>
