## About these files

Twelve trainer parts printed in PETG, the stiffer filament used for mounts and clips:

- **Fuselage lid:** `Fuselage-Lid.stl`.
- **Motor and controls:** `motor-mount.stl`, `motor-mount-nut.stl` and `Control-horn.stl`.
- **Wing:** `wind-support-V2.stl` (wing support), `wing-band-clip-front.stl` and `Wind-band-clip-rear.stl`.
- **Tail clips:** `empanage-clip-foam-tail.stl` (foam tail), `empanage-clip-3mm-ply.stl` (3 mm plywood tail), and the V-tail clips `empanage-clip-v-tail-30.stl` (30°), `Empannage-clip-v-tail-45.stl` (45°) and `Empannage-clip-v-tail-45-foam.stl` (45°, foam tail).

The fuselage, nose and servo mount are printed in [64D TPU](/resources/trainer-tpu-parts/). Editable versions of every part are in the [Fusion part designs](/resources/trainer-fusion-parts/).

The trainer parts were updated to the current designs on November 14, 2026. These downloads preserve the original file contents and filenames. Visit the [aircraft project page](/uas/trainer-airplane/) for the related components.
