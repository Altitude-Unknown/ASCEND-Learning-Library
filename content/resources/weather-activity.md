## About this activity

Decode the supplied METAR reports and decide what the weather means for UAS missions at each airport.

Answer on paper, in your own notes, or in the Word file below. Work individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions behind each answer.

## Before you start

Treat the METARs and chart as supplied historical examples, even where a question says “current.” A METAR alone does not settle every operational or airspace question.
