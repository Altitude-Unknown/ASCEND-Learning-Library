## About this resource

Decode the supplied METAR examples and discuss weather conditions for UAS missions.

Work through the supplied prompts individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions used in your answers.

## Notes for using the supplied material

Treat the METARs and chart as supplied historical examples, even where the worksheet says “current.” A METAR alone does not settle every operational or airspace question. The source spells Los Angeles as “Los Angelas.”
