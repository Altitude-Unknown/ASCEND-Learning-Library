## About this activity

**Goal:** install and set up an FPV camera, transmitter, and receiver system.

**Materials:** FPV camera, video transmitter, video receiver, video display, wire, solder, heat shrink, and a solder station.

Read the [FPV sections of Chapter 7](/camera-video-systems/textbook/camera-and-video-systems/#first-person-view-fpv-cameras) first. The [soldering section of Chapter 2](/uas-hardware/textbook/hardware-and-electronics/#how-to-solder) covers the connections.

<p class="notice"><strong>Check the voltage first</strong>Be careful not to over-voltage either the camera or the transmitter. Many cameras run on only 5 V and may need a voltage regulator.</p>

## Procedure

<ol class="activity-questions">
<li>Connect an FPV camera to a video transmitter with appropriate power for both the camera and the transmitter. Be careful not to over-voltage either the camera or the transmitter. You may need a voltage regulation circuit for the camera, as many are only 5 V.</li>
<li>Pair the transmitter to a receiver and connect the receiver to your display screen. Match the channels until you get a live video stream on your display.</li>
<li>Demonstrate your working system. Instructor signature ________</li>
<li>Attach the FPV system to an aircraft. Make sure that it is secure and won’t interfere with aircraft control, weight and balance, or cause any other undesired conditions. Instructor signature ________</li>
<li>With the help of a safety pilot (flight instructor) on dual controls, perform an FPV flight. Instructor signature ________</li>
</ol>

Follow the FAA rules that apply to your flight; an FPV pilot needs a visual observer or safety pilot who keeps the aircraft in sight. The [FAA Part 107](/part107/) course explains the rules.
