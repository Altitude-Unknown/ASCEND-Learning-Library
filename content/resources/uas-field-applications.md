## About this resource

Plan a mapping or inspection mission using five supplied real-world locations and research prompts.

Work through the supplied prompts individually or with your college team, then discuss your reasoning with a faculty mentor. Record the sources and assumptions used in your answers.

## Notes for using the supplied material

Have the faculty mentor set the exercise date and time in place of “today at noon.” Research the assigned coordinates, access, weather, airspace, and mission limits rather than treating a scenario as permission to fly.
