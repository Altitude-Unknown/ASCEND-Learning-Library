## About this activity

Put the maneuvers together into a full left-hand traffic pattern, decide when to go around, and land. These are the skills for the second to fifth airplane checkrides.

Practice each maneuver in a simulator such as [Flight Lab RC](/software/) until you can fly it without crashing, then fly it outdoors with your instructor, ideally on a buddy box so they can take over. Fly in light winds, and launch and land into the wind.

<p class="notice"><strong>Built from the Maneuver Description Guide</strong>This practice plan arranges the standards from the Maneuver Description Guide in Chapter 3 into a checklist. Your instructor sets the altitudes and limits for your aircraft and flying field.</p>

<p>Sketch a left-hand traffic pattern for your flying field before you fly, and mark the wind direction, each leg, and your touchdown zone. Your instructor will use the <a href="/resources/fly-checkride-rubrics/">airplane checkride rubrics</a> to assess your pattern and landings.</p>
