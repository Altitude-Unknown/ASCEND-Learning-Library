## Work through this unit

1. **Watch.** Start with the lesson video for this unit. If it has none yet, go straight to the reading.
2. **Read and clarify.** Read the Rules for every balloon, Restricted areas and Subpart D, and Checking your launch site and path sections of the chapter. Each rule is quoted exactly, then explained.
3. **Apply your understanding.** Check your launch site and predicted path on the FAA sectional chart or in ASCEND Flight Control.
4. **Check and revisit.** Answer the review questions in your own words, then open each answer to compare. Return to the reading for any question you missed.
