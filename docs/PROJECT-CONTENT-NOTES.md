# Project-content editorial notes

The project owner authorized using the proposal as background for public
science, goals, and organizational descriptions, explicitly excluding the
proposal document itself from the website and downloads. The original remains
outside the project intake. It is not a resource record, asset, attachment, or
public link. Public copy is selected and rewritten, not a document conversion.

## Source mapping

- `content/about.md`: proposal sections 2.1, 2.2, and 2.5; full ASCEND name,
  five goals, learning objectives, research participation, lasting capacity.
- `content/science.md`: sections 2.4.1 and 2.4.3–2.4.7; overarching question,
  all seven RQs, and complementary measurement platforms.
- `content/project.md`: sections 2.1.2, 2.4.8, 2.5, and 2.7; roles, technical
  and regional pods, learning progression, and planned data sharing.
- Homepage and topic introductions summarize those same project aims and
  link readers to the relevant explanations.

## Editorial decisions

The owner's current clarification governs the audience: teams consist of
community college and four-year university students, led by faculty mentors;
university pod leads mentor groups of teams and help run ASCEND. Do not
reintroduce K–12 recruitment language from the proposal.

Research questions are framed as investigations, not proven conclusions.
Planned campaigns, datasets, GIS maps, and repositories are described as
planned. No recruitment totals, award details, administrative arrangements,
internal evaluation procedures, partner identities, or schedule commitments
were promoted to public operational promises. No proposal attachment or full
extracted text is stored in this repository.

The public summaries omit unverified numerical performance claims, specific
calibration endorsements, and detailed hypotheses. In particular, they do not
assert EPA certification of a consumer sensor or nighttime satellite
capabilities. A scientific review of detailed protocols remains separate from
this public-facing overview.
